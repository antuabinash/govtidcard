What is MSM (ଏମ.ଏସ.ଏମ.)?

MSM is the monthly cluster-level meeting (ମାସିକ କ୍ଲଷ୍ଟର ସ୍ତରୀୟ ବୈଠକ). Teachers, CRCCs and head teachers meet once a month to discuss classroom practice, check the learning ladder register (ଶିକ୍ଷଣ ସୋପାନ ରେଜିଷ୍ଟର), take part in a gallery walk, and receive a task for the next meeting.

This page keeps the monthly PDF and a short list of key points in one place, so you can revise before the meeting or share the points with teachers who could not attend.

How do I share the key points?

Tap a month at the top, then tap Share. All its key points and the Sikhyak Sathi link go into a message that you can send on WhatsApp or any other app. Copy copies the same text.

Where do I find the PDF?

Choose a month at the top and tap Open PDF. It opens in Google Drive, where you can read it or download it.

Are the key points official?

They are short notes made from the monthly meeting material. Sikhyak Sathi is an independent portal, so please read the PDF and follow your official circulars. Tell us about any mistake on the contact page.
