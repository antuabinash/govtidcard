What is the annual plan (ବାର୍ଷିକ ଶୈକ୍ଷିକ ଯୋଜନା)?

The annual plan splits the school year into months. For each class it lists the working days, the learning days, and the periods and chapters to be taught in every subject, such as Odia, English, Maths, EVS and Social Science.

Teachers use it to prepare lesson plans, check that the syllabus is on schedule, and share the same plan with head teachers and colleagues.

Using it on your phone

  • Tap Open PDF to read it in Google Drive.
  • Turn your phone sideways so the wide table fits the screen.
  • Pinch to zoom in on a month or subject.
  • Tap Download PDF to keep a copy on your phone.
How do I save the plan?

Choose your class and tap Download PDF. The file goes to your Downloads folder, and you can share it from there on WhatsApp.

The PDF won't open. What should I do?

Try Open PDF in a new tab, or sign in to your Google account if Drive asks. If it still fails, tell us on the contact page.

Why does a class say "Soon"?

Plans are added class by class. A class shows Soon until its PDF is published here.

Is this the official plan?

Sikhyak Sathi is an independent portal. Please check the plan against your school's official circulars, and tell us about any mistake through the contact page.
