Made for everyday school work

Resize a photo for an online form, join scanned pages into one PDF, check the length of a speech or paragraph, tidy text copied from a document, or write an amount in words for a bill or cheque.

Everything runs on your own phone or computer. Your photos and text are never uploaded.

Are these tools free?

Yes. No sign-up and no limits.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They are processed on your device and never leave it.

How do I make a photo under 50 KB?

Open Photo Resizer, choose the photo, tap the 50 KB button, then tap Resize and Save.

Does it work for Odia text?

Yes. The word counter and text tools work with Odia and English.
