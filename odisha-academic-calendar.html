    About the Odisha school academic calendar 2026-27

    This calendar lists the month-by-month schedule for Odisha schools from April 2026 to March 2027: preparation and recapitulation, club and house activities, Parent-Teacher Meetings, staff meetings, national days and celebrations, and all holidays and vacations.

    Some entries are marked "verify". Please confirm those dates with your school's official circular.

    When do schools reopen after the Puja vacation?

    Puja vacation is listed from 17 to 26 October 2026. School reopens on 27 October.

    When is the Winter vacation?

    23 to 31 December 2026. School reopens on 01 January 2027, as listed.

    When are the Parent-Teacher Meetings?

    18 July 2026, 15 October 2026 and 16 January 2027.

    How do I get these dates on my phone?

    Tap Add to calendar to download a file with every dated activity. Open it on your phone and choose your calendar app.
