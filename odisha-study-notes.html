Study Zone

Chapter notes in Odia, ready to read or print

The Study Zone turns each chapter into three quick study aids: a summary (ସାରାଂଶ), the key points (ମୁଖ୍ୟ ବିନ୍ଦୁ) and questions with answers (ପ୍ରଶ୍ନୋତ୍ତର), arranged class by class and subject by subject.

Read on your phone, make the text larger, copy it into a message, or print it on ruled paper for revision.

What do I get for each chapter?

A chapter summary, a list of key points and practice questions with answers, all in Odia.

Can I print the notes?

Yes. Open a chapter, pick the notes you want and tap Print. Only the notes page is printed. You can also choose Save as PDF in your print dialog.

Can I change the text size?

Yes. Use A+ and A- above the notes. Your choice is remembered on this device.
