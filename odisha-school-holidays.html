About the Odisha school holiday list 2026

This is the holiday schedule for elementary, primary and secondary schools in Odisha (ଛୁଟି ତାଲିକା), with 82 days in total. It includes the long Summer Vacation (38 days), Puja Vacation (8 days) and Winter Break (8 days).

The 2 local holidays are decided by the respective District Collectors. An Observance is a special day marked on the list. Please follow your school's circular for the programme on that day.

When is the Summer Vacation?

06.05.2026 to 17.06.2026.

When is the Puja Vacation?

17.10.2026 to 26.10.2026.

When is the Winter Break?

23.12.2026 to 31.12.2026.
