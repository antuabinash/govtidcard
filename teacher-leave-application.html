Leave applications in a minute

Choose casual, medical, earned, maternity, paternity, child care or special casual leave, or ask permission to come late or leave early. The number of days is counted from your dates and the letter is written in simple, polite English.

Add your signature once and it is remembered on your phone. Everything is made on your own device and nothing is uploaded.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no limit.

How do I send it on WhatsApp?

Tap Share image and pick WhatsApp. If your browser cannot share directly, the image is saved to your phone and you can attach it in WhatsApp.

Can I print it?

Yes. Tap Print or PDF, then choose Save as PDF or your printer. Print on A4 paper and sign by hand if you prefer.

Is my signature safe?

It never leaves your device. Use Remove under Signature or Clear saved details to delete it.
