Questions

Parents often ask

Is Odia KidsApp free?

You can download it free from Google Play. The listing notes that in-app purchases are available.

What age is it for?

It is rated 3+ on Google Play and designed for young learners who are starting to explore language, numbers and creativity.

Are there ads or strangers my child can talk to?

No. There are no external ads, and the “Mo Sathi” buddy is a simulated, offline companion – there is no live chat or contact with real-world users.

What permissions does it need?

None. The app doesn’t require any permissions, and the developer has declared that no data is collected or shared with third parties.

Which languages does it teach?

Odia and English, with a language selector on the home screen.
