Free Odisha text books, Class 1 to 10

Browse Odia medium text books published for Odisha schools by SCERT Odisha and the Board of Secondary Education, Odisha: Odia readers, English, Hindi and Sanskrit books and grammar, Maths (Ganita, Algebra and Geometry), Science, Social Science, Art, and Health & Physical Education.

Use the class and subject filters, or search by book name, to find the right Odisha school book PDF (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ପାଠ୍ୟ ପୁସ୍ତକ) in seconds.

How do I download a book?

Pick your class, find the book and tap Download PDF. The file goes to your phone's Downloads folder. A green tick shows on books you have already downloaded.

Which classes are available?

Odia medium books for Class 1 to Class 10, covering all main subjects.

Can I save my favourite books?

Yes. Tap the bookmark on any book, then use the Saved filter. Your saved list stays on your own device.

The download did not start. What now?

Check your notification bar first. Otherwise tap the eye icon to open the book in Google Drive and download it from there.
