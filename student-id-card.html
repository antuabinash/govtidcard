Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you order.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The minimum order is 30 pieces. Larger orders get automatic bulk discounts.

How long does delivery take?

5–7 days after design confirmation. A flat delivery charge of ₹150 applies per order.

Why are lanyards charged in batches of 30?

Digital print lanyards are sublimation-printed in batches of 30. If your quantity is not a multiple of 30, you still pay for the full batch of lanyards, so ordering in multiples of 30 gives the best value. The order screen shows you exactly how much extra it costs to complete the batch.

Can I print my own logo and design?

Yes. Send your logo, text and colours on WhatsApp and we will share a preview before printing.

How do I pay?

After you send your order on WhatsApp, we confirm the design and share payment details there.
