About Sikhyak Sathi (ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ)

A free, fast toolkit for the teachers of Odisha, in Odia and English, on any phone.

Why we built it

Teachers in Odisha spend hours every week on lesson plans, forms, calendars and paperwork, often on a phone with a weak connection. Sikhyak Sathi brings these everyday tools into one place so you can spend that time teaching.

It is an independent project. It is free to use, needs no sign-up and keeps your own details on your own device.

Where the content comes from

Text books, learning outcome documents, calendars and holiday lists are based on public documents of the Government of Odisha and its departments. We gather them in one place for convenience. All rights in those documents belong to their respective owners.

Dates and circulars can change. Please always check the latest official circular before you act on anything you read here.

If you own a document shown here and would like it changed or removed, or you spot a mistake, please write to us. We will look into it promptly.
