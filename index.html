Sikhyak Sathi (ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ) – Odisha Lesson Plan, Odia Text Books, Academic Calendar & Holidays

Welcome to Sikhyak Sathi (sikhyaksathi.in), the complete digital toolkit for Odisha government and primary/high school teachers. Easily create and download daily Odisha lesson plans (Odia lesson plan / ଶିକ୍ଷାଦାନ ଯୋଜନା), access Class 1 to 10 Odisha text books and Odia text books PDF (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ପାଠ୍ୟ ପୁସ୍ତକ), check the official Odisha academic calendar and 2026 Odisha school holidays list (ଛୁଟି ତାଲିକା), track OCF learning outcomes, calculate PM POSHAN MDM rations, and connect directly to HRMS Odisha, OSEPA, UDISE+, and e-Shishu portals.

About This Portal

ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ (Sikhyak Sathi) is a comprehensive, all-in-one digital companion designed exclusively for the dedicated educators of Odisha.

Our mission is to dramatically reduce your daily administrative workload by providing a lightning-fast Lesson Plan Generator, structured study resources, rapid access to HRMS/OSEPA/UDISE+ portals, and essential utility tools.

Built with offline-first capabilities, this platform works wherever you are, ensuring that you can focus more on what truly matters: teaching your students.

🛡️

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our absolute priority.

Zero Server Storage: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ operates primarily as a localized PWA (Progressive Web App). All locally generated data—such as your saved Lesson Plans, School Name, and Profile details—are securely stored entirely inside your device's offline memory (Local Storage).

We do not track, upload, sell, or monitor your personal data. Your workspace remains entirely yours.
