Everything an Odisha teacher needs, in one place

Sikhyak Sathi (ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ) is a digital toolkit for Odisha government and private school teachers. Create daily Odisha lesson plans (Odia lesson plan / ଶିକ୍ଷାଦାନ ଯୋଜନା), browse Class 1 to 10 Odisha text books and Odia text books PDF, revise with chapter-wise study notes, check the Odisha academic calendar and 2026 school holidays list (ଛୁଟି ତାଲିକା), follow OCF learning outcomes, and calculate PM POSHAN mid-day meal rations.

Built to be fast on any phone and to keep working when the network is weak.

What is Sikhyak Sathi?

A set of tools and resources for Odisha teachers: lesson plans, text books, study notes, learning outcomes, calendar, holiday list, leave letters and calculators.

Does it work with a weak internet connection?

Yes. It is a Progressive Web App. Install it to your home screen and it is designed to keep working when your connection is slow or unavailable.

Where is my lesson plan and school data stored?

Only on your own device, in local storage. It is not uploaded to a server.

Is this an official government website?

No. It is an independent teacher-support portal, not affiliated with the Government of Odisha, SCERT or OSEPA. Always confirm dates and rules with official circulars.

How do I install the app?

On Chrome for Android, tap the Install button at the top of the page. On other browsers, open the browser menu and choose “Add to Home screen”.

About this portal

ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ (Sikhyak Sathi) is an all-in-one digital companion designed for the dedicated educators of Odisha.

Our mission is to reduce your daily administrative workload with a fast Lesson Plan Generator, structured study resources and essential utility tools.

Built with offline-first capabilities, it keeps working wherever you are, so you can focus on what matters most: teaching your students.

Privacy policy

Your privacy and data security are our priority.

Zero server storage: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ works primarily as a Progressive Web App. Data you create, such as saved lesson plans, school name and profile details, is stored inside your device's own memory (local storage).

We do not upload, sell or monitor your personal data. Your workspace stays yours.
