About This Portal

ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ (Sikhya Sathi) is a comprehensive, all-in-one digital companion designed exclusively for the dedicated educators of Odisha.

Our mission is to dramatically reduce your daily administrative workload by providing a lightning-fast Lesson Plan Generator, structured study resources, rapid access to HRMS/OSEPA portals, and essential utility tools.

Built with offline-first capabilities, this platform works wherever you are, ensuring that you can focus more on what truly matters: teaching your students.

🛡️

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our absolute priority.

Zero Server Storage: ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ operates primarily as a localized PWA (Progressive Web App). All locally generated data—such as your saved Lesson Plans, School Name, and Profile details—are securely stored entirely inside your device's offline memory (Local Storage).

We do not track, upload, sell, or monitor your personal data. Your workspace remains entirely yours.
