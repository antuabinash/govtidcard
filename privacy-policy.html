Privacy Policy

What we collect, what stays on your device, and the choices you have. Written in plain language.

Last updated: 2 November 2026
In short: there are no accounts. We do not sell your data. Your details, photos, signatures and text are handled on your own device. We only receive what you choose to send us, such as an email or an ID card order.

1. Who we are

Sikhyak Sathi (ଶିକ୍ଷକ ସାଥୀ) at sikhyaksathi.in is a free, independent website and web app for teachers of Odisha. You can reach us at andigital1996@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your own device

The tools remember some things in your browser's local storage so you do not have to type them again. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it and it is not sent to our servers. It can include:

  • Your light or dark mode choice and recently used tools.
  • Details you save in tools, such as lesson plan settings, school name, class choices and profile details.
  • Leave Writer details such as your name, designation, school, place and a signature image, if you add one.
  • Markers such as which learning outcome PDFs you have downloaded.

Photos, signatures and text you give to our tools (for example the Photo Resizer, Images to PDF or Leave Writer) are processed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to us.

The app also keeps a copy of its pages on your device so it can work with a weak or no connection.

3. Information you choose to send us

  • Email and messages. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us. We use it only to reply and to improve the site.
  • WhatsApp. Some pages (such as ID card orders) open WhatsApp with a ready-made message. The message is created on your device and is sent only when you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to your chat.
  • ID card orders. If you order ID cards, the details and photos you share are used only to prepare and deliver your order.

We keep emails and messages only as long as needed to answer you or to complete an order, and as long as the law requires.

4. Other services our pages use

To work, some pages load files or data from other services. When your browser contacts them, they can see standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as with any website. They have their own privacy policies.

  • Google Fonts for fonts on some pages. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Drive for some text book PDFs.
  • GitHub for some PDF downloads, such as learning outcomes. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare cdnjs for a script that turns your letter into an image. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase for data used by the Test Plan and Print Test tools.
  • Google Analytics (Google Tag Manager script) for anonymous visit statistics. How Google uses data
  • Google Play when you open a link to our Odia KidsApp. The app's Play Store page has its own information.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data. The site uses your browser's local storage and an offline cache (described above), which are not used to track you across other websites.

Analytics: we use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate, which pages are visited and how many people use the site, so we can improve it. It may set cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type. IP addresses are anonymised, and we do not use advertising features or Google signals. We never send what you type into our tools, such as names or letter text, to analytics. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by turning on Do Not Track in your browser, which this site respects.

The other services listed in section 4 may also set their own cookies. If we add advertising in future, we will update this page first.

6. Children

This website is made for teachers and other adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

  • Delete what is on your device. Clear this site's data in your browser settings, uninstall the app from your home screen, or use "Clear saved details" in Leave Writer.
  • Ask us. You can email us to ask what we hold about you from your messages or orders, or to ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Stop at any time. You do not need an account, so you can simply stop using the site.

8. Security and changes

The site is served over HTTPS. No website can promise perfect security, so please do not enter information you are not comfortable sharing.

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email andigital1996@gmail.com or use the contact page.
